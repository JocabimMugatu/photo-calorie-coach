Setup

Copy these prompts. Your coach configures itself.

Send these to Fo by text after you sign up. Swap in your own numbers, times, and goal. Say it in your own words if you prefer: this is the shape that works.

1

Set your goal and start the food log (send once)

"I'm starting photo calorie tracking. My goal is 2,000 calories a day and losing 10 pounds. From now on, every meal photo I send: estimate calories and macros, log it with the time, and keep a running total for the day."

2

Lock in the log format

"Log every meal as: time, what you saw, calories, protein, carbs, fat, and my running total. If a photo is unclear, ask me what I'd add before you guess."

3

Set the meal nudges

"If I haven't sent you a meal photo by 1pm, nudge me. Same again at 7pm."

4

Set the morning check-in

"Every morning at 8am, check in with me: recap yesterday's total against my goal, ask me one question about how I'm feeling, and remind me to photo every meal today."

5

Set the evening recap

"Every night at 9pm, recap my day: calories vs my goal, whether I hit my protein, and one specific fix for tomorrow."

6

Set the weekly recap

"Every Sunday at 6pm, send my weekly recap: days logged out of 7, average daily calories, my weight trend if I've shared it, and one fix for next week."

7

Add the check-in call (optional)

"Call me every weekday at 7am for a two-minute check-in on my food log. Keep it short and keep me honest."

8

Send your first meal

Then just text a photo of your plate. That's the whole service.

Honest

What it is, and what it isn't

An accountability engine

The daily check-in, the nudges, the weekly recap. That's the part that actually changes behavior.

An estimate, not a lab

Photo estimates are good at recognizing food and weaker at portion size, and hidden calories like oils and dressings get undercounted. Treat the numbers as a running score.

Not medical advice

Fo is not a dietitian or a doctor. Talk to yours about any health condition or plan.

The human part stays human

Fo is the closest thing to a body tutor that fits in your pocket, but no AI is a real human coach. If you want one, hire one. Fo makes the days between sessions count.

Does it really cost nothing?

Yes. Fo's free plan covers this. You sign up with the link and start the same day. No card, no trial, no upsell.

Does Fo actually call me?

It can. Prompt 7 schedules a real check-in call at whatever time and days you set.

How accurate are the calorie numbers?

Good enough to track trends, not good enough to bet a medical plan on. Consistency in logging matters more than decimal-point precision.

Can I use it for macros and weight goals too?

Yes. Prompt 1 frames every estimate and recap around whatever goal you set: cutting, bulking, protein floor, whatever you're chasing.

What happens to my food photos?

They're messages in your own chat with your own assistant. Nothing is posted anywhere and no one else sees your feed. Fo doesn't use your chats to train third-party AI models.

What's the catch?

None. Fo's assistant has a free plan, and this page just teaches you the prompts that turn it into a food-log coach. If you ever want a bigger plan, that's your call. This workflow runs on free.
